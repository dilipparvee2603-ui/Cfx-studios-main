Cookie Policy

An overview of cookies and similar technologies that may be used on CFX Studios websites.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Similar technologies may store preferences, support security or measure website usage.

How cookies may be used

  • Essential: support core site functions, security and network delivery.
  • Preferences: remember choices such as display or language settings, where available.
  • Analytics: help understand traffic and site performance, if analytics tools are enabled.

Third-party services

Embedded content, analytics, hosting or external links may involve third-party technologies governed by those providers’ policies. CFX Studios does not control cookies set by external websites.

Managing cookies

You can usually block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of a website function.
