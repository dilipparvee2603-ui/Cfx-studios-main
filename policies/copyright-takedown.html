Copyright & Takedown

How rights holders can report material they believe infringes their copyright or other rights.

Respect for intellectual property

CFX Studios respects intellectual property rights and expects visitors and contributors to do the same. Do not submit or use material unless you own it, have permission, or have another lawful basis to use it.

Submit a takedown request

If you believe material accessible through a CFX Studios website infringes your copyright or other rights, email dilipparvee@gmail.com with the subject “Copyright / Takedown Request”.

Please include:

  • Your name and contact details, and your relationship to the rights holder.
  • A clear identification of the copyrighted work or protected material.
  • The exact URL(s) of the material you are reporting.
  • An explanation of why you believe the use is unauthorized.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are authorized to submit it.

Review and action

We may request additional information, restrict access to reported material while reviewing a notice, or take other appropriate action. Submitting a notice does not guarantee removal. Knowingly false or misleading notices may have legal consequences.

Counter-notice or response

If you believe material was removed or restricted in error, contact us with the relevant URL, an explanation and supporting information. We will review the response as appropriate.
